Our company traces its roots back to 2010 in Siliguri, West Bengal, and since then, we’ve never looked back. With numerous successful projects completed, we proudly stand as a trusted name in the sweet industry.

For over a decade, our agency has delivered a wide range of sweet and architectural services across West Bengal and nearby regions. We continue to grow while upholding our values of quality, commitment, and client satisfaction.
We simplify the sweet process with complete in-house solutions—from design to execution—removing the need to coordinate with multiple teams.
